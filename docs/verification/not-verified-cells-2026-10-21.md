## C. A cell that a new source contradicts (fix before submission)

**California, ordinary partnership (Table 1 "Share of profits" and Table 3 "Partner in an ordinary firm"; v14: "Voidable at common law (not read)").**
CDTFA Annotation 410.0067.750 (8/20/03), https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol2/suta/410-0000-all.html (live, 21 Oct 2026): "A minor is prohibited from being a partner in a California partnership, under Family Code 6701, because he or she lacks the legal capacity to give a delegation of power and a delegation by a partner to the other partners is required for partnership under California law."
Fam. Code § 6701: "A minor cannot do any of the following: (a) Give a delegation of power." (project copy `raw\ca_fam_6701.txt`, leginfo via IA.)
**Proposed cell:** "Barred in the state tax agency's view (Fam. Code § 6701(a); CDTFA Annotation 410.0067.750); no case read".
This is an agency opinion, not a court holding. The same delegation reasoning may extend to LLC membership (#5), and a reviewer who knows California will raise it. Adding it also strengthens the paper's argument: like India, California reaches a bar through a general capacity rule rather than a partnership statute.
